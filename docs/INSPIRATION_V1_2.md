## Design decision: powerful yet low-noise

- Source/API contracts, static review and previously collected HAR evidence
  precede any approved network contact.
- Every public outbound HEAD/GET operation retains the v1.1 exact-host
  policy and TLS validation; v1.2 adds stop-on-401/403/429/5xx.
- No stealth, scanning camouflage, agent-directed network pivoting,
  rotating proxies, user-agent impersonation or WAF bypass.
- Local Ollama Qwen only sees **bounded aggregate task metadata** in v1.2.
  It cannot choose an arbitrary tool, insert a command or grant itself scope.
- A reporter may prioritize a source correlation, but it may not certify
  exploitability, bounty eligibility, CVE issuance or zero-day status.
