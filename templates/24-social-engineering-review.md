# HunterQA — private Social Engineering worksheet

Copy outside this repository. Never commit completed copies, names, contact lists,
recipient exports, domains, agreements, SMTP credentials or submitted values.

1. Record owner authorization, local evidence scope, exercise provenance, approved
   audience/channels, reviewer and review date. Keep actual participant details private.
2. Review content approval, participant protection, avoidance of credential/data
   collection, reporting paths, emergency stop/escalation, privacy/retention and debrief.
3. Independently review current mail-authentication policy, actual receiver behavior,
   simulation transport configuration and scope. Saved DMARC/config declarations
   are not proof of live enforcement, consent or effective protection.
4. Review non-email controls through independent observations: identity verification,
   voice/SMS scope, physical-access and removable-media policies, stop conditions
   and follow-up. No contact, payload or physical action is automated by this pack.
5. Fill JSON observations: true = supported assurance review, false = assurance
   gap, null = unassessed. Keep supporting references in this private worksheet.
6. Optionally review a saved DMARC value or minimized GoPhish results object.
   Record migration/default/receiver limits and unknown outcome labels. Counts
   describe source rows/latest states, not unique people or cumulative funnels.
7. Independently triage candidates, record remediation owner and authorized retest.
   Do not rank individual employees or claim training effectiveness from metadata;
   scanners/proxies and incomplete telemetry can affect outcomes.
