# HunterQA — private Utility worksheet

Copy outside this repository. Never commit client findings, scope identifiers,
paths, evidence values, credentials, notes or completed deliverable records.

1. Record owner authorization, local report/evidence scope, provenance/date,
   reviewer and the approved reporting/delivery context.
2. Review scope/budget, coverage gaps, evidence triage, false-positive controls,
   approval before active validation and justified handoff priority.
3. Review executive narrative, impact, source evidence, metric justification,
   remediation, retest state, redaction, limitations and delivery approval.
4. Fill assurance values: true = supported review, false = assurance gap, null =
   unassessed. Keep supporting private references here only.
5. Optionally review saved finding metadata. Presence of evidence references or
   a confirmed label does not establish source integrity or a validated finding.
   Inspect duplicate-context suggestions manually; never delete findings solely
   because metadata matches or absence of a suggestion implies uniqueness.
6. For selected CVSS 3.1 Base arithmetic, justify every metric independently and
   keep score/vector together when publishing. Other versions and Temporal/
   Environmental scoring need separate tooling. Arithmetic is not business risk.
7. Review generated quality JSON/Markdown privately; it is not a replacement
   client report or delivery approval. Record remediation owner and authorized retest.
